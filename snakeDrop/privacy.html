Snake Drop for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Snake Drop doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your games stay on your device.

What the app stores

Snake Drop keeps the following on your device so the game can work:

  • Your best score
  • Your chosen start level and whether music is on
  • Whether you've bought Unlock All Levels

This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you've turned on iCloud Backup, Apple may include it in your device backup, as with any app's data. That backup is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. Snake Drop makes no network connections of its own and contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising or crash reporting. We can't see your scores or how you play.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Snake Drop sends your score to Apple's Game Center leaderboard at the end of each game, so you can compare scores with other players. Your Game Center nickname and score may be shown to other players on the leaderboard. Game Center is run by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your Apple Account details.

Game Center is optional. If you don't sign in, you can still play every part of the game, just without the leaderboard. You can sign out in Settings > Game Center.

Purchases

Unlock All Levels is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the extra levels. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Snake Drop is suitable for all ages. Because it doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting Snake Drop from your device removes all of its data. Your purchase stays linked to your Apple Account, so you can restore it with Restore Purchases if you reinstall. Scores already on the Game Center leaderboard are managed by Apple.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Snake Drop ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email tom@writelogic.co.uk or visit the Snake Drop support page.
