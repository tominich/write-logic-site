Splat Duo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short: Splat Duo has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data to serve and measure them. Buying “Remove Ads” switches the ads off, and the ad software stops loading.

Who we are

Splat Duo (“the app”) is made by Tom Nicholas (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for this app and this policy. You can reach us at tom@writelogic.co.uk.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your phone so it works the way you left it:

  • Your top 10 scores, with the number of bugs squished and the date of each game
  • Whether background music is on or off
  • Whether you have bought “Remove Ads”

We never receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard, where other players can see it next to your Game Center nickname. Apple runs Game Center under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out or change your Game Center settings at any time in the Settings app.

In-app purchases

“Remove Ads” is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card or Apple Account details. The app only checks with Apple whether you own the purchase, so it can hide the ads and restore your purchase on another device.

Advertising

Until you buy “Remove Ads”, the app shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads between games, served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix crashes, the AdMob software may collect:

InformationExamples
Device identifiersApple's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and the app-specific vendor identifier (IDFV)
Approximate locationYour general area, estimated from your IP address
Ad interactionsWhich ads were shown and whether you tapped them
DiagnosticsCrash reports, performance data, device model and operating system version

Google handles this information under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the app may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you say no, Google cannot use your advertising identifier and you will see less personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (UK, EEA and Switzerland): If you are in one of these regions, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time by tapping “Privacy choices” on the title screen.
  • No ads at all: After you buy “Remove Ads”, the ad software is no longer started and no advertising data is collected.

Legal basis (UK and EEA users)

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies, personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw through “Privacy choices”. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and crash reporting rely on our legitimate interest in keeping a free app running and working properly.

Children

Splat Duo is a general-audience game and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Keeping and deleting information

We do not run servers or keep any of your information ourselves. Information on your device is deleted when you delete the app. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies. You can also reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we hold none of your information, requests about advertising data are best made to Google, but we are happy to help. Just email us. If you are in the UK or EEA, you can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tom@writelogic.co.uk.
